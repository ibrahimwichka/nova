# Synthetic clear-bag fixtures

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. They show food in a transparent plastic bag, not a tray. Use a ticket with exactly 1 Burger, 1 Fries and 1 Sprite. They are synthetic smoke-test data and do not establish field accuracy.

- complete.jpg: one visible burger, one fries carton and one readable Sprite can inside a clear takeaway bag.
- missing-drink.jpg: one visible burger and one fries carton inside a clear takeaway bag, no drink.

Prompt set: realistic smartphone overhead diagonal photo of one transparent clear takeaway delivery bag on a plain counter, handles visible and loosely open, gentle plastic wrinkles, all food clearly visible without overlap, no tray or unrelated objects, natural light. Complete variant requests exactly one sesame cheeseburger, one red fries carton and one green 330ml Sprite can with readable branding. Missing variant requests exactly one burger and one fries carton with an empty right side and no can, bottle or drink.

Packing: open AI camera packing check, then Guided photo demo. Analyze the missing image. Next adds the Sprite and invokes a fresh real OpenRouter analysis. At the house, repeat in Check the delivery photo. You can also download either image and upload it yourself through Upload photo / Upload delivery photo.
